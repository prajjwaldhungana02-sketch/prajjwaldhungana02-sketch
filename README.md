# Hi, I'm Prajjwal Dhungana 👋

Development economist from Nepal. MPA candidate at **Princeton SPIA** (Economics and Public Policy, SPIA Graduate Fellow), Graduate Associate at the Julis-Rabinowitz Center for Public Policy & Finance, and Environmental Policy Associate at the Center for Policy Research on Energy and the Environment.

I use **earth observation, open-source intelligence and causal machine learning** to measure what development projects actually change, with a focus on **energy, water and climate security** and **state capacity** in South Asia.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pradhu99/) · [REAR Econ](https://rearglobal.com.np/) · [Princeton profile](https://jrc.princeton.edu/people/dhungana)

## What I work on

| Area | Questions | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| **Climate, energy & water security** | Do renewable energy and water investments create jobs and resilience in climate-exposed, fragile places? How does climate change drive migration and inequality? | Satellite data (Landsat, Sentinel-2, VIIRS), spatial difference-in-differences, double ML, SEM |
| **State capacity & public sector performance** | Do governments and development banks learn from their own evaluations? | LLMs on project documents, text embeddings, event studies |
| **Next:** crisis response, responsible AI | How do fragile states respond to shocks? How should AI be used in public institutions? | OSINT, AI evaluation |

**Background:** three years of applied research before Princeton. At ICIMOD I was a Senior Quantitative Economics Associate (climate-induced migration, adaptive capacity and precarity across four countries) and a SANDEE pre-doctoral associate (economics of forest restoration in Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka). Before that, I was a researcher at Kathmandu University on climate change and income inequality. I consulted for the Micronutrient Forum (global systematic review on climate and nutrition) and 3ie (evidence gap maps on anaemia and on climate and biodiversity). BA Economics, Kathmandu University (3.99/4, Nepal Bidhya Bhusan Medal).

**Tools:** Stata · R · Python · QGIS · Google Earth Engine · EPPI-Reviewer · LaTeX

## 📢 REAR Econ

[REAR Econ](https://rearglobal.com.np/) is a research initiative I founded in 2025 under Rear Global (Lalitpur, Nepal). It helps aspiring applied economists, especially from low-income and first-generation backgrounds, grow into policy-engaged researchers through hands-on research, mentorship and policy engagement. About 330 aspiring researchers have taken part, and I have mentored 6 through a closed research program.

- **AEMM: Applied Economics Is More Than Money**: a storytelling series showing that economics is about human behavior, not only money. [Posts](https://github.com/prajjwaldhungana02-sketch/rear_econ_posts/tree/main/aemm), e.g. why the timing of flood cash mattered after the Bhotekoshi flood.
- **TLAE: Thinking Like An Aspiring Economist**: video interviews with aspiring economists about their paths.
- **TWG: Thematic Working Group**: a reading and discussion group that produces evidence briefs (vocational training, menstrual products and school attendance, education reform).
